## 1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use StudentSphere, we may collect the following categories of information:

- **Account and contact information.** Your first and last name, email address, credentials used for authentication, email verification status, and account-security preferences.
- **Profile and community information.** Profile photograph, banner image, biography or headline, selected university or other community affiliations, city and state you provide, skills, interests, roles (such as student, prospective student, staff representative, or ambassador), communities you follow, and your connections.
- **Content and communications.** Forum posts, questions, answers, comments, uploaded images, polls and votes, event responses, messages, reports, feedback, and requests to add communities. Content may include information about other people that you choose to share.
- **Student or staff verification information.** If you request a verified status, we may collect the verification method, school or organization, role or position, student or staff identification images, photographs of you with identification, tuition or enrollment documents, reviewer decisions, and related records. These materials may contain sensitive identifiers. Please obscure unrelated account numbers and other information when our instructions permit.
- **Support and moderation information.** Messages sent to our support team, content reports and reasons, moderation history, appeals, and records needed to investigate misuse or respond to requests.
- **Usage and device information.** IP address, approximate location inferred from an IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referral pages, timestamps, pages or features used, session records, and security or error logs.
- **Settings and integrations.** Privacy preferences, messaging permissions, notification preferences, active sessions, and information needed to connect optional third-party integrations if you choose to use them.

We receive most account, profile, verification, and content information directly from you. Other information is generated when you use the Service or is provided by members who interact with you. Where integrations are offered, we may receive limited information from the connected service as described at the time of connection.

## 2. How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

1. To create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts, including email confirmation, two-factor authentication, session management, and password recovery.
2. To display profiles, community memberships, posts, conversations, events, polls, and other features you request.
3. To personalize feeds, suggest relevant communities or connections, and remember your choices.
4. To process student or staff verification requests and administer trusted roles and permissions.
5. To deliver messages, replies, notifications, service announcements, and requested support.
6. To review reports, enforce community standards, prevent spam or fraud, and protect the safety and integrity of the Service.
7. To monitor performance, diagnose technical problems, assess aggregate use of features, and improve the Service.
8. To comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

If we wish to use your personal information for a materially different purpose, we will provide additional notice and obtain consent when required by law.

## 3. What other people can see

StudentSphere includes public and community-facing features. Your display name, selected profile details, community affiliation, and contributions may be visible to other members or visitors depending on the feature and your settings. Forum posts, questions, comments, and similar content may be reshared or captured by others even after you remove them.

Where available, Account Settings allow you to control email visibility, profile discoverability, and who may initiate direct messages. We encourage you to review these settings before posting or contacting others. A privacy setting may limit discovery or contact without making all previous content private.

Direct messages are intended for their participants, but **they are not represented as end-to-end encrypted**. We may access or disclose message content when reasonably necessary to provide the Service, investigate abuse or security concerns, comply with law, or protect users. Other participants may save or share messages outside StudentSphere.

**Verification documents are not public profile content.** Access to them is intended to be limited to you, authorized personnel evaluating verification, and service providers acting on our instructions.

## 4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to:

- **Other users and visitors**, when you publish content, choose to make profile information visible, follow or interact with communities, or send messages.
- **Moderators and authorized reviewers**, to manage communities, resolve reports, review verification applications, investigate abuse, and enforce rules. Access should be limited to what their responsibilities require.
- **Service providers**, such as vendors providing hosting, data storage, authentication, email delivery, security, diagnostics, or customer support, subject to appropriate contractual safeguards where required.
- **Optional connected services**, when you initiate an integration or follow a link outside StudentSphere. The third party's terms and privacy practices may then apply.
- **Authorities or other recipients when legally necessary**, including to comply with valid legal process, enforce our agreements, prevent fraud or imminent harm, or defend rights and property.
- **A successor organization**, if StudentSphere is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of assets, with appropriate notice and protections as required by law.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party targeted advertising. We do not disclose identification or verification documents for advertising purposes. If our practices change, we will update this Policy and provide choices or obtain consent where legally required.

## 7. How long we keep information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including operation of your account, ongoing conversations, community integrity, safety, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and enforcement. Different categories may require different periods:

- **Account and profile data:** generally while your account is active, then as needed to process deletion and meet legal or security obligations.
- **Posts and interactions:** while published, unless removed by you or us; limited records or copies may remain for moderation, integrity, legal, or backup reasons.
- **Messages and reports:** for the periods necessary to operate messaging and resolve safety or abuse concerns, subject to an internal retention schedule.
- **Verification documents:** subject to the specific schedule in Section 5.
- **Security and technical logs:** for limited periods proportionate to their operational and security purpose.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it, subject to reasonable backup cycles and legal holds.
